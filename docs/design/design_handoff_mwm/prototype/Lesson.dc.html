What you’ll learn

How to solve a pair of simultaneous equations where one is quadratic — for example x² + y² = 25 with y = 2x − 5. You’ll substitute the linear equation into the quadratic, solve for x, then find both pairs of solutions. Every step is written out, including the factorising.
